Red 3s are bonus cards: 100 points each when your team has melded (800 for all four), or −100 each if it has not. They are not part of a meld. These values also count against a team for cards left in its hands.

Make melds of 3+ equal cards. At least two must be natural, and a meld can contain at most three wild cards (2s and Jokers). Seven cards make a Canasta. Red 3s are laid aside and replaced immediately after the deal, and score in the team's red 3s row; black 3s can only be melded when going out.

A team may have only one meld of each rank, so matching cards are added to it. Click a team meld to add staged cards to it. Your team's first meld must reach the threshold shown in the status line, unless you meld your whole hand with a Canasta. The discard pile may be frozen or blocked — the game will say which. A wild card or red three anywhere in the pile freezes it, even after later cards cover it; the freezing card is shown turned at right angles in the pile.

Melds show coloured ranks, with jokers as stars and wildcards on the right. A canasta of seven or more cards shows a count. Taking the pile uses the cards it needs from your hand: two matching naturals, or one match and a wild unless the pile is frozen. It can also add the top card to your team's matching meld. Stage cards to choose them yourself.

Coach — the COACH light bulb at the bottom right stays dim while the Coach is on and lights up when it has a better scoring line. Hover or click it for the plan; H turns the Coach on or off.
